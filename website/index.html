FAQ

Common Questions

Why is the AI roast so mean?

Because typical fitness trackers tell you you're doing great even when you eat junk foods and skip sleep. FitApp's AI is programmed to give you the raw, unfiltered truth inside the Future You forecast tab, keeping you accountable by projecting the long-term impact of your current daily choices.

How does the 5-second scanner work?

We leverage Gemini multimodal AI models to visually parse your meal photo, estimating portion weight, identifying ingredient ratios, and computing macro information (calories, protein, carbs, fat) in five seconds. You can also scan barcodes or raw nutrition labels.

Does it sync with Apple Health?

Yes. FitApp hooks directly into Apple Health (HealthKit) to automatically import your active workouts, steps count, and sleep duration. It uses this data to update your daily quest progress without requiring manual entries.
